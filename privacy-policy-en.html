Voiced Telegram / Telegram вголос

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how the Voiced Telegram Android application (also named Telegram вголос, package com.voicedtelegram; the “app”) handles data. The developer and privacy contact is Mykola Varanytsia. Email: boomsya@gmail.com.

1. Why data is processed

The app reads aloud new text messages from Telegram chats, groups and channels you select. Data is used to sign in to an existing account, display chats, filter and speak messages, remember preferences, diagnose problems, show advertising and verify an ad-removal purchase.

This is an independent app, not an official Telegram product. It does not create a separate developer account or register new Telegram accounts.

2. Sign-in and Telegram data

When you start signing in, the app communicates with Telegram servers using TDLib. Authentication involves your phone number, verification code and, if required, two-step verification password. Telegram also receives connection information such as your IP address, device model, system and application version, and language.

The app receives information about available chats and Telegram contacts, including identifiers, titles or names, photos, chat types, read status and new message text. Your chat selection determines which messages are subsequently processed for speech. If you enable the relevant option, the app sends read acknowledgements to Telegram.

Authentication data is sent to Telegram, not to a separate developer server. The app does not add your phone number, sign-in code, password, chat titles or message text to its own analytics events. Secret chats are not supported. Telegram's own privacy policy governs its processing.

3. Speech and voice downloads

Message text is processed to apply phrase filters, remove phrases, normalize pronunciation and generate speech. A chat title or contact name may be included if you enable source announcements.

With the local Supertonic 3 neural engine, speech is generated on your device after model files have been downloaded. Text and generated audio are not sent to a cloud TTS service for this synthesis. Text queues and audio buffers are temporary; the app does not create its own archive of spoken messages or audio recordings.

With Android TTS, the text to be spoken is passed to your installed system TTS service. Processing may be local or online depending on the provider, voice and settings, and is subject to that provider's policies. To use local synthesis within this app, use the downloaded neural voices.

Neural model files are downloaded from Hugging Face and its delivery infrastructure. These services receive your IP address and technical download-request information. Message text is not included in model download requests.

4. Data stored on your device

Private app storage holds preferences, selected chat identifiers, stop-phrase and phrase-removal lists, schedules, languages, voice settings, downloaded models and purchase verification data. TDLib also stores Telegram session data, chat information and cached downloaded photos.

TDLib's persistent message-history database is disabled in this app. This does not mean that no local data is stored: session information, chat information and the other items described above remain. Text required for current processing may temporarily be held in memory.

For its core functionality, the app does not request microphone, camera, precise location or device address-book access. Contact information displayed in the interface comes from Telegram. Background speech uses a playback service and a system notification.

5. Google Analytics for Firebase

The app automatically sends technical data and usage events to Google Analytics to analyze its operation and identify problems. These include an installation identifier, device information, app version and operation outcomes. We do not add phone numbers, sign-in codes, passwords, chat titles or message text to our own analytics events. Use of these data for advertising personalization through Analytics is disabled.

The SDK also processes standard usage and session information, app version, device and OS information, an app-instance identifier and approximate location derived from network information. These are pseudonymous data, not a guarantee of complete anonymity.

Analytics events may include screens visited, sign-in step outcomes, error categories, selected languages and engine, operation duration, approximate text-length categories, counts of selected chats or processed messages, voice-download outcomes, and purchase or restore outcomes. Phrase-list content and complete purchase records are not added to our own events. Advertising-ID collection for Analytics is disabled in the app configuration. This setting does not disable AdMob’s separate data processing.

6. Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics, including support for native libraries, to identify and fix crashes. Reports may include stack traces, exception types and messages, crash timestamps, process state, technical device and OS information, app version, installation and session identifiers, and technical events preceding a crash.

We attach technical context such as language, speech engine, component, operation and connection state. Conversations, sign-in codes and passwords are not dedicated report fields. Automatic diagnostics and library error messages may contain additional information about the state at the time of a failure. These reports are used for diagnostics and reliability improvements.

7. AdMob advertising and your choices

Unless ad removal has been purchased, the app may display Google AdMob banners. The advertising SDK processes IP addresses, which may indicate approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID when available. These data are used for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics and fraud prevention, and may be shared with Google's advertising partners according to applicable settings and Google's policies.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) obtains and applies advertising choices where required by the service configuration and applicable requirements. When UMP requires access to a choice-management form, the corresponding option is available in the app's settings. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings.

An ad-removal purchase removes banners once the entitlement is verified. It does not turn off Analytics or Crashlytics or delete previously collected data. UMP advertising choices are likewise not a separate Analytics or Crashlytics switch.

8. Google Play purchases

Google Play handles payment. The app does not receive your card number, CVV or full payment credentials. Google Play supplies information needed to verify a purchase, including the product identifier, purchase token, signature, status, time and, when available, order identifier.

A signed purchase record is stored locally to verify and restore ad removal. Purchase, cancellation and failure outcomes may be recorded in analytics without full payment details. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data does not cancel a purchase or delete Google Play's transaction records.

9. Recipients, security and international processing

Depending on the feature, recipients include Telegram, Google and its service providers (Firebase, AdMob and Google Play), your chosen Android TTS provider, and Hugging Face and its model-delivery infrastructure. If you contact support, we receive your email address and the content of your request. Do not send Telegram codes, passwords, card credentials or unnecessary private conversations.

We do not sell your message content. The app does not upload a message archive to a separate developer server. Service providers may process data outside your country under their policies and applicable data-protection arrangements. Disclosure may also occur in response to lawful requirements from competent authorities.

Safeguards include Telegram's secure connection protocols, HTTPS/TLS for relevant Google services and model downloads, Android private app storage, model integrity checks and purchase signature verification. No device or communication channel can be guaranteed completely secure. Ordinary Telegram cloud chats should not be confused with end-to-end encrypted secret chats.

This privacy-policy webpage is hosted on GitHub Pages. When it is visited, GitHub logs and stores the visitor’s IP address for security purposes under its privacy statement. We do not add advertising or analytics scripts to the policy webpage.

10. Retention

Local preferences, models, caches and purchase verification data remain while needed for the app or until you clear its storage or uninstall it. Temporary text queues and audio buffers are used during processing and playback without creating a separate archive.

The selected retention period for detailed Google Analytics user and event data is 14 months. Deletion follows Google Analytics' processing cycle; standard aggregated reports may remain longer. This period is not the retention period for all data held by every service.

According to Firebase documentation, Crashlytics retains crash stack traces, extracted diagnostic data and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. Telegram, AdMob, Google Play, Android TTS providers and Hugging Face apply their own retention policies and legal obligations.

Support correspondence is kept while needed to answer and resolve the request and, when necessary, to comply with legal obligations or resolve a dispute, and is then deleted.

11. Control and requests

You can change selected chats, delete phrase lists, stop speech and sign out of Telegram in the app. Signing out ends this client's authorization and resets chat selection, but does not delete your Telegram account or history and does not clear all app preferences.

To remove this app's local data, clear its storage or uninstall it through Android settings. If needed, also terminate its session through Telegram's device settings. Use Telegram's own tools to delete your Telegram account or server-side data, and the relevant Google tools for Google data and payments.

The current version has no separate user switch for Analytics or Crashlytics. Stopping use and uninstalling the app stops further SDK operation from this installation, but does not automatically delete data already transmitted.

For requests to access, correct, delete or restrict data, object to processing or withdraw consent where these rights apply, contact boomsya@gmail.com. We will consider requests within the period required by applicable law and explain available actions. We do not link analytics identifiers to your Telegram phone number or email, so an email address alone may not identify a particular pseudonymous record. We cannot promise deletion of data outside our control or records that must legally be retained. You may also contact a competent data-protection authority.

12. Age requirements and policy changes

Use requires an existing Telegram account and compliance with Telegram's current age requirements and applicable law. Contact us if you believe that a child's data has been processed contrary to these requirements.

This policy may change as features, services or requirements change. The current version is published on this page with its update date. Material changes will be communicated appropriately, and where renewed consent is required, it will be requested before the relevant processing.
